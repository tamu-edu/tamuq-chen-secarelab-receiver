The heat transfer and flow properties of the receiver are investigated, and a design rule of the gradual structure is summarized. The volumetric effect is achieved by using the gradual structure receiver. Such novel volumetric solar receiver with gradual structures achieves the volumetric effect, boosting the solar to thermal efficiency to 87%. Although parameters of each layer can be discretionarily selected based on the optical depth, higher porosity of the gradual structures allows for higher efficiency."
